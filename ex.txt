# Product Photoshoot Direction for AI Note Taker App

## COMPOSITION STYLES
Off-center subjects with negative space - Place users in lower third or side of frame, letting empty space suggest contemplation
Architectural framing - Use doorways, windows, concrete overhangs to create geometric frames around subjects
Profile shots and averted gazes - Avoid direct eye contact; capture users looking down at device or gazing thoughtfully away
Bird's eye view angles - Overhead shots of hands interacting with device on clean surfaces
Intimate close-ups with shallow depth - Focus on hands, partial faces, device screens with heavy background blur
Diagonal lines and urban geometry - Use stairs, railings, building edges to create dynamic leading lines
## LIGHTING
Overcast window light - Soft, diffused natural light through large windows on cloudy days
Golden hour side lighting - Warm, low-angle sun creating dramatic shadows and highlighting device screens
Harsh architectural shadows - Strong directional light creating geometric shadow patterns on subjects
Moody tungsten interiors - Warm indoor lighting mixed with cool daylight for color temperature contrast
Single key light setups - Dramatic studio lighting with strong shadows, minimal fill
Reflected light off concrete/marble - Using urban surfaces to bounce soft, cool-toned illumination
## CAMERA & TECHNIQUE
Leica M-series or Fujifilm X-Pro - Rangefinder aesthetic with subtle film emulation
Shoot on Kodak Portra 400 or Tri-X - Actual film for authentic grain and color rendition
50mm and 35mm focal lengths - Classical documentary focal lengths, avoid wide or telephoto extremes
Square format crops - 1:1 aspect ratio referencing medium format film photography
Intentional motion blur - Slow shutter speeds capturing movement and gesture
High contrast black & white processing - Push shadows deep, keep highlights clean
## LOCATIONS
Tadao Ando-inspired concrete spaces - Minimalist concrete interiors with clean geometric lines
Empty museum galleries - White walls, polished concrete floors, dramatic ceiling height
Brutalist university libraries - Raw concrete, repetitive architectural elements, study carrels
Modern office building lobbies - Marble surfaces, floor-to-ceiling windows, minimal furnishing
Rooftop terraces with city views - Urban skylines as backdrop, concrete planters, metal railings
Underground parking garages - Fluorescent lighting, concrete columns, geometric repetition
Empty subway platforms - Clean modernist transit architecture, tile walls, metal benches
## CLOTHING
Lemaire oversized wool coats - Architectural silhouettes in camel, charcoal, or olive
Uniqlo U basics - Clean cotton turtlenecks in cream, black, or muted earth tones
Acne Studios tailoring - Minimalist blazers and trousers with subtle construction details
COS knitwear - Structured sweaters in neutral palettes, interesting texture without decoration
Norse Projects outerwear - Technical minimalism, clean lines, muted color blocking
Vintage Helmut Lang pieces - 90s minimalist precision, monochromatic palettes
Accessories: Wire-frame glasses, simple leather goods, analog watches (Braun, Uniform Wares)
Color palette: Black, white, cream, charcoal, olive, camel - avoid bright colors or busy patterns
## MOOD DIRECTION Capture users in moments of genuine contemplation - looking thoughtful while taking notes, pausing mid-thought, or reviewing captured ideas. The app should feel like a sophisticated tool for serious thinkers, not a casual consumer product. Think "architect reviewing blueprints" rather than "student cramming for exams."

# PRODUCT PHOTOSHOOT DIRECTION

## COMPOSITION STYLES

Liminal Documentation Style
Off-center framing with subjects positioned in negative space
Capture app usage during transitional moments (waiting in lobbies, empty offices after hours)
Use reflective surfaces (windows, phone screens, mirrors) to create layered compositions
Frame shots through doorways, under stairwells, or in corner spaces
Intimate Surveillance Aesthetic
Close crops on hands holding phones with harsh flash falloff
Over-the-shoulder shots showing partial app interface with user's environment bleeding in
Mirror selfie compositions where the app is visible but secondary to the mood
Document actual workflow interruptions - note-taking during real conversations
## LIGHTING

Clinical Flash Photography
Direct on-camera flash creating harsh shadows and overexposed foregrounds
Fluorescent office lighting mixed with phone screen glow
Golden hour window light cutting through sterile interior spaces
Sodium vapor street lighting for nighttime usage scenarios
Analog Film Grain Quality
Shoot with actual film or heavy grain simulation
Push film/digital to create authentic texture and imperfection
Use available light sources rather than professional lighting setups
## CAMERA APPROACH

Kodak Portra 400 pushed to 800 for grain and color cast Contax T3 or similar point-and-shoot for authentic flash aesthetic If digital: Ricoh GR III for that clinical documentation feel iPhone with flash for genuine social media documentation style

Avoid: Professional DSLRs, perfect focus, color correction

## COMPOSITION STYLES

Primary Style: Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Elements
Frame subjects in contemplative, slightly melancholic poses - looking away from camera, absorbed in thought
Use asymmetrical compositions with negative space to create intellectual breathing room
Layer analog textures (papers, books, fabric) around digital interface to show cultural collision
Shoot through objects: vintage glass, architectural elements, natural materials
Create diptychs showing both the digital interface and the user's physical environment
Use Dutch angles and unconventional cropping inspired by avant-garde fashion photography
Secondary Style: Liminal Space Documentation
Capture transitional moments - user pausing mid-thought, stylus hovering over screen
Show hands in intimate detail interacting with technology
Frame the app within larger environmental context that feels atmospheric and moody
## LIGHTING

Golden Hour Melancholy
Soft, directional window light during overcast conditions
Warm tungsten practicals mixed with cool daylight for emotional depth
Dramatic chiaroscuro inspired by Renaissance painting techniques
Hazy, diffused light through sheer curtains or frosted glass
Backlighting that creates silhouettes and rim lighting on subjects
Avoid harsh digital-looking LED setups - everything should feel analog and warm
Specific Setup:
Key light: Large softbox positioned 45 degrees off-camera
Fill: Bounce card with warm gel
Practical lights: Vintage desk lamps, candles, warm Edison bulbs
## CAMERA & TECHNICAL SPECS

Camera: Film or Film-Emulated Digital
Shoot on 35mm film (Kodak Portra 400 or Fuji 400H) for authentic grain
If digital: Sony A7R IV with film emulation LUTs in post
Medium format film (Mamiya RZ67) for hero shots requiring maximum detail
Lenses:
50mm f/1.4 for intimate, natural perspective
85mm f/1.8 for compressed, dreamy portraits
24mm f/2.8 for environmental context shots
Technical Approach:
Shoot wide open (f/1.4-f/2.8) for shallow depth of field
Embrace slight motion blur and imperfection
Film grain is essential - never clinical digital sharpness

# PRODUCT PHOTO SHOOT AESTHETIC GUIDE

## COMPOSITION STYLES

Documentary Intimacy Approach
Shoot from slightly elevated angles capturing users in natural, unguarded moments
Use tight crops showing hands interacting with phones, emphasizing tactile connection
Frame subjects through doorways, windows, or architectural elements for voyeuristic authenticity
Employ asymmetrical compositions with subjects positioned in rule-of-thirds placement
Include environmental storytelling elements (coffee rings, worn notebooks, vintage objects)
Cinematic Portrait Style
Medium shots showing torso and hands, reminiscent of 1990s editorial photography
Use negative space to create contemplative mood
Layer subjects against textured backgrounds (exposed brick, weathered concrete)
Employ shallow depth of field to isolate subjects from busy backgrounds
## LIGHTING

Golden Hour Documentary
Shoot during late afternoon with warm, directional window light
Use practical lighting from vintage desk lamps, Edison bulb fixtures
Create film noir shadows with strong directional lighting
Employ available light from neon signs, cafe windows, gallery spaces
Analog Film Aesthetic
Slightly underexpose to achieve that coveted film grain look
Use warm color temperature (2700K-3200K) to mimic tungsten film stock
Create subtle lens flares and light leaks in post-production
Employ high contrast lighting with deep shadows and bright highlights
## CAMERA & TECHNICAL SPECS

Equipment:
Fujifilm X-T5 or X100V (for that analog aesthetic without actual film costs)
35mm and 50mm prime lenses only (no zooms - too clinical)
Shoot in Fujifilm's Classic Chrome or Classic Neg film simulation modes
Add subtle grain in post-processing
Settings:
Shoot wide open (f/1.4-f/2.8) for shallow DOF
Use natural light whenever possible
Capture in RAW but process to look like scanned film
Slight vignetting and color grading toward warm tones

Domestic Spaces:
